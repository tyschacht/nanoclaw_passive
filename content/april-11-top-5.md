## #1 — Hyper-Niche Etsy Digital Products (ADHD Sub-niches)

*Category: Marketplaces | Agentic Score: 8/10 | Timeline to first sale: 2–4 weeks*

### What It Is

AI generates hyper-specific digital products for underserved sub-niches on Etsy. Platform handles all fulfillment automatically. The insight: specificity is the moat, and AI is uniquely good at exhaustive enumeration of niche combinations that humans would never patiently research.

### Why It's #1

*Validated with real current data:*

- ADHD sub-niches (e.g., "ADHD cleaning planner"): *28/100 keyword difficulty*, *+3,210% YoY search growth*, 14,800 monthly searches, median $6.20 price. Top listings pull ~240 sales/month per listing.
- The broad "ADHD planner" term is locked by FutureADHD (9 years, 60,000+ sales). The sub-niches are wide open.
- Average Etsy digital shop nets *$489/month profit* (Customcy 2026 aggregate). Top performers doing $2,000–$5,000/month have 30+ listings and 6–12 months of reviews.
- Window is *6–18 months* before sub-niches saturate at current competition growth rate.

*Platform handles the hard parts:* automatic file delivery on purchase, built-in payments, built-in search traffic. Once listed, this is genuinely passive.

### What Validation Found (Honest)

- Generic AI products are heavily saturated — "AI art" flooded the market and buyers actively avoid AI-looking products in generic categories
- Etsy's June 2025 AI policy update: must disclose AI use, must use original prompts (not purchased prompt bundles), bulk uploads of 50+ similar listings trigger suspension
- HTML files are not officially supported — deliver as .zip (adds friction vs. PDF)
- Revenue takes 6–12 months to compound through reviews and search ranking

### Action Plan

1. *This week:* Run niche enumeration — use Claude to generate 50+ combinations of [ADHD variant] × [specific use case] × [format]. Cross-reference against Etsy search result counts.
2. *Generate 10–20 products* for the best sub-niches (ADHD cleaning, ADHD hormone tracking, ADHD college students with late diagnosis). PDF format first — skip HTML until you have traction.
3. *List 10 products* — Etsy CSV import for batch listing; Claude writes all SEO copy.
4. *Review at 30 days* — any listings with views but no sales need A/B test on thumbnails or titles; any with sales get doubled down.

## #2 — Autonomous Micro-SaaS (The Pixel Pattern)

*Category: Software | Agentic Score: 8/10 | Timeline to first revenue: 4–8 weeks*

### What It Is

Claude Code acts as an autonomous developer: builds a product, monitors competitors, scrapes feature gaps, proposes improvements. Human role: niche selection and a weekly approve/reject session. Everything else is agent work.

### Why It's #2

*What's real:* The technical capability is fully validated. A first-person case study (cutler.sg) documents Claude Code autonomously converting a 19-year-old, 87-post WordPress blog to NextJS in 26 minutes of runtime, costing $7.99. Claude spawned parallel agents (Analyzer, Builder, Migration Architect, QA) and resolved TypeScript errors across 6,020 lines of generated code with zero human intervention mid-run.

The cost savings are verifiable: dev shop quotes of $10,000–$20,000 upfront + $2,000–$5,000/feature vs. $200/month Anthropic subscription. The build cost is essentially eliminated — leaving only niche selection and marketing as human work.

*Revenue ceiling:* Micro-SaaS at $500–$3,000/month is achievable. The highest-validated path is a niche-specific automation tool (not "AI for X" broadly) that solves a specific painful workflow for a well-defined audience.

### What Validation Found (Honest)

- Revenue claims from YouTube ($30K/month for "Unfundable Clips") are unverified — not reproducible as a benchmark
- PMF (product-market fit) is the actual hard work — Claude can build anything, but building the wrong thing wastes time
- Maintenance burden exists even for autonomous apps — major failures need human response
- Competition is increasing rapidly as the build barrier drops for everyone

### The Niche Selection Problem

This is the only critical bottleneck. Best sources for finding the right problem:
- r/SideProject and r/SaaS "I wish there was a tool that..." threads
- r/Entrepreneur complaints about specific operational pains
- "Boring" automation: compliance docs, billing disputes, scheduling — these have paying customers with clear ROI

### Action Plan

1. *Before building anything:* Spend one week on problem research. Read 50+ Reddit complaints in a specific vertical. Find 3 people who would pay $50/month right now.
2. *Write PRODUCT.md:* Target user, core job-to-be-done, what success looks like.
3. *MVP in one session:* Claude Code → full-stack scaffold → Vercel deploy → Stripe integration.
4. *Get to 3 paying customers before adding the agent loop.* Proof of demand first.
5. *Layer automation:* Competitor scraping, feature gap proposals, monitoring — after revenue exists.

## #5 — Polymarket Market-Making Bot (Kalshi for US Users)

*Category: Finance | Agentic Score: 9/10 | Timeline to first revenue: 3–6 weeks*

### What It Is

An automated bot providing liquidity on prediction market exchanges. *Not* directional betting ("will Bitcoin go up?") — that's negative EV. *Market-making:* posting limit orders on both sides of a market, earning the spread, and collecting maker rebates. The bot runs continuously and generates income proportional to capital deployed.

### Why It's #5

*The markets are real and validated:* Polymarket launched 5-minute Bitcoin markets February 12, 2026. Within one month: $60M/day volume, 288 separate 5-minute markets cycling per day. 15-minute and hourly markets for BTC, ETH, SOL also active. Chainlink oracle settlement. Full CLOB API available (py-clob-client, Node.js clob-client).

*The market-making path is the correct one:* Market makers pay zero fees and receive daily USDC rebates funded by taker fees. 100% of taker fees flow to makers. One documented example: $4,000–$5,000 capital in limit orders = ~10% monthly return net = $400–$500/month. This is consistent with the original Sharbel A. claim and provides a mechanistic explanation for how it's achieved.

### Why It's #5 and Not Higher

*Two concrete blockers:*

1. *US geoblocking:* Polymarket's international platform (where 5-minute markets live) is geoblocked for US IP addresses per the 2022 CFTC settlement. The US platform (prediction.markets) is invite-only waitlist as of April 2026 — full public access estimated Q3–Q4 2026. US alternative: Kalshi, which has hourly crypto direction markets with a full API — but only hourly, not 5-minute.

2. *Capital requirement:* Meaningful returns require $4,000–$5,000 deployed. $400–$500/month on $5,000 capital = 8–10% monthly return — real, but requires actual cash at risk.

3. *Retail directional betting = loss:* 98% of Polymarket traders have never made $1,000 total. Round-trip taker fees = 3.12%. Bots exploit oracle latency and eat retail takers alive. The $400–$500/month figure requires the market-making approach specifically.

### Action Plan

*For US-based users:*
1. *Start with Kalshi:* Full public API, US-legal, hourly BTC/ETH direction markets. Get familiar with CLOB mechanics.
2. *Build a simple market-maker:* Post limit orders at bid-1 and ask+1, collect spread when matched, rebalance positions hourly.
3. *Paper trade 2 weeks* with simulated positions to validate the spread capture logic.
4. *Deploy $1,000 initially* — understand your actual returns before sizing up.
5. *Monitor quarterly for US Polymarket launch* — when 5-minute markets open to US users, migrate the bot.
