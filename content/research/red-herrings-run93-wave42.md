# Wave 42 Bundle — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Four signals from the June 8 2026 research sweep that appeared potentially promising but each fails the agentic passive income filter.

## Why Each Doesn't Work Agentically

### 1. Gemini CLI / Antigravity Transition (NOT an income mechanism)

Google is sunsetting the open-source Gemini CLI on June 18, 2026, replacing it with Antigravity CLI (closed-source, enterprise-focused). Multiple tech outlets covered this as a "bait-and-switch." Some YouTube content pitches "use Gemini CLI to earn passive income" — but Gemini CLI is a dev *tool*, not an income *platform*. There is no built-in revenue mechanism in Gemini CLI or Antigravity. The SKILL.md format is still supported in Antigravity, so the broader skill ecosystem continues — but the "Gemini CLI income" angle specifically is misleading.

- **Scores**: Agentic 0/10 (not an income mechanism), Overall 2/10.

### 2. Emergent.sh — Resolves June 7 WATCHLIST

The WATCHLIST entry `emergent-platform-side-projects` (June 7) flagged Emergent.sh as a possible revenue-sharing marketplace. **Resolved: it is not.** Emergent.sh is a no-code AI app builder (builds React/Python/MongoDB apps from natural language). It's a development tool — users build their own products and handle Stripe payments themselves. Emergent does not take a rev share cut, does not provide a creator marketplace, and has no programmatic listing API. The "$30k over 2 years" referenced in the Reddit post was a user's own product revenue, not Emergent income.

- **Scores**: Agentic 2/10 (it's a dev tool, not income platform), Overall 3.5/10.

### 3. AXIVIO "Sleep Stack" (YouTube, zS7pckvbKDY, June 7 2026, 10 views)

A brand-new YouTube video claiming "I Built an AI Agent That Earns $300/Day While I Sleep (Full Setup Tutorial)." The described mechanism: three AI agents that (1) find leads, (2) write personalized outreach emails, and (3) follow up automatically. This is a **lead generation + cold email service model** — not passive income. The revenue comes from clients paying for the leads/outreach service, which requires ongoing client acquisition, account management, and deliverable quality control. Zero verified earnings. 10 views at time of research = zero community validation.

- **Scores**: Agentic 5/10 (some automation possible), Setup 6/10, Yield 4/10 (requires clients), Risk 6/10 (cold email gray area). Overall 5.25/10. **Fails agentic viability threshold.**

### 4. AI Wealthy — "This New AI Voice Agent Pays $1,500/Client" (YouTube, rW_xbKBly24, June 8 2026, 0 views)

Yet another iteration of the after-hours AI voice receptionist agency model. $1,500/client claim, voice AI for local businesses that "miss 60% of calls." This is the same pattern as:
- `retell-ai-voice-receptionist-service` (evaluated previously)
- `tampa-bay-plumbing-speaknode-case-study` (NOT_VIABLE, Run 84)
- `red-herrings-run84-wave33` (SpeakNode + Latenode + Housecall Pro blocked)

Voice AI services **require client acquisition** (calling local businesses, pitching, onboarding) which is irreducibly human. The agent delivers the ongoing service but cannot close the initial sale. Service model, not passive income. 0 views = zero community signal.

- **Scores**: Agentic 5/10 (delivery agentic, acquisition not), Overall 5.25/10. **Fails agentic viability threshold.**
