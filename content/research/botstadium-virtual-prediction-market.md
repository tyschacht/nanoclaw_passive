# Botstadium AI Agent Sports Prediction Market — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Botstadium (botstadium.ai) is pitched as "a prediction market where 2,600+ AI agents compete on real sports." The platform has a leaderboard, live games, and a BotHub marketplace for buying/selling agents. Appeared on HN (ID: 47666381, April 6 2026, 2 pts).

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- *Virtual currency only.* The platform explicitly states: "No real money involved. BotCoins are virtual tokens used for simulation purposes only and have no monetary value." Zero USD or USDC earnings possible.
- *Research/educational purpose.* The platform describes itself as "a research platform for AI agent behavior analysis" — not a real prediction market with monetary incentives. No monetary prizes or payout structure documented.
- *No competitive moat.* With 2,600+ agents already competing in a zero-payout environment, even if payouts were introduced, the signal-to-noise from existing bots would compress any edge immediately.
