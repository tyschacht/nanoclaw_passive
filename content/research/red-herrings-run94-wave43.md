# Run 94 Wave 43 — Not Viable Bundle

## Items Evaluated

### 1. KDP Automated Workbooks — Harm/Ban Risk Confirmed

**Source:** r/passive_income post "I built a passive income stream generating workbooks, but I just realized it might be causing real harm" (score 120 comments / 65+ upvotes on top comment, June 2026)

**Claim:** AI-generated KDP workbooks earning $900/month since January 2026. System: script + AI generates niche daily reflection journals/habit trackers, formats them, creates covers, uploads to KDP. Fully automated pipeline.

**Why it fails (new data adds to existing red herring):**
- Confirms KDP automated upload pipeline IS technically achievable and earning real money ($900/mo)
- New risk data: OP posted about a mental health workbook (exposure therapy for social anxiety) generating sales — without any psychology background
- Community response overwhelmingly: "Take it down immediately — lawsuit exposure + Amazon will ban your entire KDP account if one customer reports a mental health product causing harm"
- Top comment (124 upvotes): "If a customer gets hurt and reports your book, Amazon will ban your entire KDP account instantly. You will lose 100% of your passive income overnight"
- Confirms: KDP revenue is real; KDP automated pipeline is real; KDP ban risk and legal liability risk are BOTH confirmed and severe
- This is additional evidence that the existing KDP red herring classification is correct — not new information but a strong public case study

**Scores:** Agentic 5 (pipeline works but fragile), Setup 5 (complex Puppeteer scraping), Yield 6 ($900/mo documented), Risk 2 (account ban + lawsuit risk for mental health adjacent content). Overall: 4.5/10. NOT_VIABLE.

### 2. Toku.agency — Another Cold-Start Agent Task Marketplace

**Source:** DEV.to article "The Agent Economy Is Real: 12 Platforms" (kirothebot, May 2026) — Toku listed as "Tier 2"

**Claim:** Fixed-price service marketplace for AI agents. Services listed at $15–$25 each (React Dashboard $15, API Documentation $20, Code Security Review $25). 15% platform fee.

**Why it fails:**
- No documented transaction volume or revenue data from any creator
- Fixed prices of $15–$25 with 15% fee = $12.75–$21.25/task net. Need 25+ tasks/week to reach $500/week — implausible on a cold-start platform
- Pattern matches "agent gig marketplace" red herring (19th+ instance). Cold-start problem documented extensively in prior runs
- No GitHub presence, no documented API, no self-service listing endpoint confirmed
- Same structural issue as dealwork.ai ($0.92/task avg on 264 completed tasks total), ugig.net (no offramp), execution.market (test tasks only)

**Scores:** Agentic 7, Setup 5, Yield 3 (no volume), Risk 6. Overall: 5.25/10. NOT_VIABLE.
