# Danube AI Tools Marketplace — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Danube (danubeai.com) is an AI tools marketplace where developers upload an OpenAPI spec or MCP server, set optional pricing, and their tools become discoverable by AI agents across Cursor, Claude Code, and other MCP clients via a single unified endpoint. The pitch: "agents can search and find your tools without users needing to manually configure anything." For developers, the promise is passive income from AI agents discovering and paying for your tools automatically. The platform launched in early 2026 and received a Show HN post (item 47501216) that reached 3 points.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Cold-start pattern (8th confirmed instance).** Danube has 100+ tools and 21 registered integrations — effectively zero buyer-side volume. The platform is in the same pre-viable position as BountyBook, SwarmDock, 0xWork, ClawGig, Claw Earn, TaskMarket, and Moltplace before it. Technical quality of the platform does not predict buyer demand. The 3 HN upvotes confirm the community has not adopted it as a discovery layer.

- **Unclear revenue model and payment rails.** The platform documentation does not specify the revenue share percentage, payout currency (USD vs USDC), minimum payout threshold, or how payments are processed. Any income claim for Danube requires these to be confirmed and functional. The "optional pricing" language suggests the majority of listed tools may remain free, which fragments the potential buyer pool further.

- **Redundant with superior alternatives already evaluated.** For developer monetization via MCP tools, the viable paths are already documented: MCPize (85% rev share, hosted), AgenticMarket (90% Founding Creator tier), mcp-marketplace.io (85% rev share, 4,300+ servers), and self-hosted via Stripe + Cloudflare Workers (97% rev share). Danube offers none of the confirmed marketplace traffic of these platforms and adds discovery-layer risk.
