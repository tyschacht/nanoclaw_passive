# Polybuild.app Polymarket Copy-Trading — Not Viable (Requires Human Judgment)

## What It Claims to Be

A YouTube video by Mr.Profit (KOTGnXqmsZI, "I made a $1025/day AI Polymarket Trading Bot (Claude Code)," April 9 2026) promotes polybuild.app, a web platform for building Polymarket trading bots. The pitch: connect a Phantom wallet, browse leaderboard copy traders filtered by recent win rate (24h, 7d), select the best-performing trader to copy, configure your bot around their signals, and collect profits autonomously. Claims $30,000/month.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- *Requires human judgment at the critical step:* The income-generating decision — which copy trader to follow — is explicitly a human editorial call. The video instructs viewers to find traders "in the game" with recent win rates, evaluate their trade history, and assess domain expertise. No automated mechanism selects or rotates copy traders based on performance decay. The "bot" executes the human's selection, it does not make it.
- *Claims are unsubstantiated engagement bait:* "$30,000/month" and "$1,000/day consistently" are stated in the first 20 seconds, before a giveaway prompt ("comment what you trade on Polymarket for a $200 giveaway"). This is a standard YouTube engagement tactic, not a verified result. Our existing notes flag this pattern: "Revenue claims on YouTube should be treated skeptically. The most credible figures are small and specific."
- *Partially covered territory:* MoneyMinions already has a VIABLE report on directional Polymarket bots (polymarket-crypto-direction-bots) and a NOT_VIABLE exploration of same-market arbitrage. Polybuild.app is a GUI wrapper for copy-trading — a less automated variant of what's already documented. It adds no novel agentic mechanism.
