# No-Code Builder Template Marketplaces — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Three major no-code / low-code builder platforms each have a marketplace where "you can publish templates and earn passive income": v0 by Vercel (the AI-first Next.js scaffolder with 20M+ generated apps), FlutterFlow (Flutter visual builder with 250K+ apps built), and Bubble.io (the web app builder with 3M+ apps). The pitch: AI generates templates for these platforms → you publish → users buy → passive income.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- *v0 by Vercel has no creator revenue sharing program.* Community templates on v0.app are free to submit and free for users to clone. There is no paid template marketplace, no revenue share mechanism, and no creator program with compensation. Vercel generates revenue from v0 usage, not from template marketplace transactions. Any "earn passive income selling v0 templates" claim is unsubstantiated
- *FlutterFlow marketplace requires fully manual submission with 30-day review.* All four submission steps require human UI interaction in the FlutterFlow editor (set project for marketplace → complete cover photo/description form → submit for approval → manage via dashboard). Review takes "up to 30 days depending on complexity." Revenue share percentage is deliberately not disclosed publicly — community forum posts confirm that when asked directly, the answer is "contact us" — a strong signal that terms are non-standard or unfavorable. Minimum prices ($50 for UI kits, $400 for full apps) are documented, but the platform's cut is opaque
- *Bubble.io plugins: €12/month average earnings, not agentic.* Market analysis shows most Bubble plugin developers earn less than $100/month, with one developer reporting just €12/month from 298 active installs. The single company that dominates (Zeroqode, 60% of marketplace revenue) has 700+ plugins and subsidizes development from agency revenue — not a standalone income model. Additionally, building a Bubble plugin requires their visual editor (human design sense required), making it non-agentic at the build step
