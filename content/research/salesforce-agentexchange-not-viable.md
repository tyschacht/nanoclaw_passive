# Salesforce AgentExchange — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Salesforce AgentExchange is the AI agent marketplace for the Agentforce ecosystem — a unified platform combining AppExchange, Slack Marketplace, and Agentforce components. It claims to accept both ISV partners *and* "individual Agentblazers with ideas," enabling anyone to publish AI actions, topic bundles, prompt templates, and full agent templates to 245,000+ Salesforce customers. With the $50M AgentExchange Builders Initiative backing it and 85% revenue share, it sounds accessible.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Partner Org required, no exceptions**: Smartitstaff.com documentation confirmed explicitly: "No, only registered Salesforce partners with access to a Partner Business Org can list solutions. Individual developers need to be associated with a partner organization." The "Agentblazer" branding is marketing language, not a self-service publishing path. You must go through the Salesforce Partner Program and sign a commercial agreement.
- **Security review is enterprise-grade**: Listing requirements include GDPR compliance, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / HIPAA certifications, Einstein Trust Layer integration, and "designated security experts." This is a 3–6 month process designed for funded companies, not solo developers.
- **Same structural barrier as Make.com and Salesforce AppExchange ISVforce**: Partner agreement gate = same pattern as Make.com Apps (Tally form, Official Partner Agreement required) and ISVforce (15% to Salesforce, commercial agreement required). None of these have ever yielded solo-developer passive income in our library.
