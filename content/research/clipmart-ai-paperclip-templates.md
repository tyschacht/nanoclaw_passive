# ClipMart.ai (Paperclip Company Templates) — Not Viable Today

## What It Claims to Be

[[Paperclip]] (paperclip.ing) is an open-source orchestration framework for "zero-human companies" — it hit 43K+ GitHub stars within weeks of its March 4, 2026 launch. ClipMart.ai is the emerging official marketplace for buying and selling pre-built Paperclip "agentic company templates": complete org charts with CEO/CTO/engineer agents, budgets, workflows, and SKILL.md files baked in. Browse templates, fork, and deploy in under 60 seconds. Template categories span Marketing, SaaS, E-commerce, Agency, Media, and Finance. Each template listing shows estimated monthly cost (~$95–$350) and estimated revenue potential ($500–$60K+/month).

A third-party marketplace, ClipMarts.com, also exists with 247+ products (skills, templates, CLAUDE.md configs, MCP bundles, workflow recipes).

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically (Yet)

- **No confirmed creator revenue share model.** The ClipMart.ai site has a "Submit template" button but does not disclose a revenue share percentage, payout mechanism, or creator terms. The only reference found — "earn commission income from 20+ programs automatically" — reads like affiliate marketing language, not template sales revenue. Until a concrete rev-share is publicly documented, this is unverifiable.
- **Platform still in early prototype.** The official Paperclip GitHub shows 11 commits, no releases. ClipMart.ai launched in prototype form and the marketplace mechanics (pricing per template, creator payouts) appear to still be in design/development. ClipMarts.com (third-party) has 247+ products listed but similarly doesn't document seller terms on its homepage.
- **Template submission process is not confirmed agentic.** If the submit flow requires a human web form with manual review, the pipeline is not fully agentic — similar to the Framer Creator Program (one-time human click required). Until an API submission endpoint is documented, we can't confirm this is truly hands-free.
- **Revenue potential figures are speculative.** The "$500–$60K+/month estimated revenue" figures shown per template are projections based on what the deployed company *could* earn — not documented earnings from template buyers. This is marketing copy, not audited data.

## What Would Make This Viable

If Paperclip/ClipMart.ai launches with: (1) documented revenue share ≥ 70%, (2) programmatic template submission via CLI or API, and (3) one or more creators documenting $200+/month from template sales — this immediately becomes a VIABLE agentic income idea. Claude Code can build complete Paperclip company templates (YAML org charts + SKILL.md + CLAUDE.md) faster than any human. First-mover advantage is significant given 43K+ GitHub stars = 43K potential template buyers.
