# Predict Engine — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

PredictEngine.ai is a no-code Polymarket trading bot builder that promises automated 24/7 prediction market trading in "30 seconds." The YouTube advertisement (kpd4iKB91qE, "Predict Engine" channel, Apr 8 2026) shows $162 earned over one month with "zero effort." The platform offers a visual strategy builder, AI assistant for strategy creation, copy trading, and arbitrage detection — all running on their cloud infrastructure.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **No documented sustained P&L.** The $162 claim covers one month and is effectively an advertisement for the platform. The "92.4% of Polymarket traders lose money" statistic (Finance Magnates, early 2026) applies equally to no-code bots running arbitrary strategies with no verified edge.
- **Strategy design requires human judgment.** Despite the "no-code" wrapper, the platform still requires users to choose a domain, configure thresholds, and select which traders to copy — the same bottleneck that made polybuild-polymarket-copy-trading NOT_VIABLE. "Create a bot around whatever you know best" is not an agentic specification.
- **Redundant with existing evaluated platforms.** Predict Engine covers the same ground as polybuild.app (already NOT_VIABLE), Creo, PolyCop copy trading, and the broader polybuild-polymarket-copy-trading category. No novel alpha source is introduced.
