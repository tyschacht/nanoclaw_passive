# 1024EX AI-Native Prediction Market — Not Viable Today

## What It Claims to Be

1024EX (1024ex.com) launched its Mainnet Alpha on February 25, 2026, billing itself as "the world's first prediction market supporting AI agent trading." The platform includes AgentX (an AI agent that accepts natural language strategy instructions and executes trades autonomously), a 1024SDK (NL → strategy → backtest → one-click deploy pipeline), and a 1024Chain proprietary blockchain for settlement. Market categories include crypto derivatives, equities, energy, FX, and event-based prediction contracts. Agentic viability in theory is very high — this is purpose-built for autonomous AI trading.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically (Today)

- **Still waitlist-gated as of April 2026.** The Mainnet Alpha launched with "limited early access" in February 2026. No public reports confirm open access. The testnet is publicly available but earns nothing. Without account access, this is not an actionable income mechanism.
- **No public fee structure.** Trading fees are not documented in any official source, press release, or third-party coverage. An income strategy cannot be constructed without knowing the cost of entry on each trade.
- **No documented trading results.** AgentX marketing claims "high-win-rate logic" but publishes zero supporting P&L data, backtests with real results, or community-reported returns. All prediction market platforms in this category (WeatherBot.finance, Polyclaw, Predict Engine) have failed this test — 1024EX is no exception until it provides real data.
- **Proprietary chain with no track record.** 1024Chain is not Ethereum, Polygon, or BNB Chain — it's a custom blockchain with no established security track record and no published audit. Capital deposited for trading carries smart contract risk that Kalshi (CFTC-regulated) and Polymarket (Polygon USDC) do not.
- **Third-party agent integration undocumented.** AgentX integration for external agents (e.g., OpenClaw via Telegram) is described in press materials but has no public SDK documentation, API reference, or working examples available. Integration feasibility is unconfirmed.

## Watchlist Criteria

Re-evaluate when ALL of the following are true:
1. Open public access (no waitlist)
2. Published fee schedule
3. Independent community reports of 30+ day live trading P&L for AI agents
4. Public API documentation for third-party agent integration
5. Smart contract audit published
