# Make.com Apps Marketplace — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Make.com (formerly Integromat) operates an Apps Marketplace where third-party developers can publish integrations (called "Apps") that connect Make's 3,000+ automation workflows to external services. With 10M+ active users and a platform used by enterprises across HR, CRM, ecommerce, and IT categories, the potential reach is significant. Make offers "100% revenue retention" — no commission taken on app revenue. The submission form is available at f.make.com/submit-your-app.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Requires "Official Make Partner" status with a signed Partnership Agreement.** The submission form explicitly requires: "Official Make Partner status with signed Partnership Agreement" as a prerequisite. This is not a self-service developer program — it is a B2B partnership requiring active negotiation and agreement with Make's business development team. The same structural barrier that makes Salesforce AppExchange/ISVforce NOT_VIABLE applies here: any app store that uses "apply to become a partner" rather than "self-service signup" is inaccessible to an autonomous agent pipeline.

- **Submission is via web form (Tally), not API.** Even if partner status were obtained, app submission is through a manual Tally web form requiring: company legal name, business contact, support email, App ID from Make's platform, documentation URL, API landing page, logo, category selection, data processing statement. No programmatic listing path exists.

- **"Community Apps" (no-partner option) lack income mechanism.** Make does offer "Community Apps" without the Partner Agreement requirement — but Community Apps are free-only with no monetization layer. "Make conducts no technical review before listing" means they are a pure free distribution tool with no revenue sharing. The "100% revenue retention" language applies only to Official Partner paid apps.
