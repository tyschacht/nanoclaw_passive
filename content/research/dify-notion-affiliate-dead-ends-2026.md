# Dify Template Marketplace & Notion Integration Program — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

**Dify Template Marketplace** (marketplace.dify.ai): Dify is an open-source LLM workflow builder with 30M+ downloads and $30M Series Pre-A raised in 2026. Their Creator Center lets anyone submit workflow templates (JSON export file upload), which are listed in the Marketplace for one-click adoption. Income model: connect a PartnerStack affiliate account in Creator Center, and when users subscribe to a paid Dify plan after clicking your template link, you earn 30–50% recurring commission for 12 months. Affiliate tier: 30% base, upgrades to 50% after first 20 paid conversions.

**Notion Integration Gallery**: Notion's partner program for approved integrations. Featured integrations must be publicly available via OAuth and pass security/privacy review. Revenue share requires a formal Technology Partner Program application AND minimum $10K ARR enterprise deals to qualify for the Consulting Partner program.

## Why They Don't Work Agentically

**Dify templates:**
- **Manual submission + human review required.** Templates are submitted via the Creator Center web dashboard ("upload an export file"). There is no REST API for programmatic template creation or bulk publishing. Every template requires a human review cycle before listing.
- **Income is affiliate commission, not template payment.** You earn nothing when someone uses your template — only when they sign up for a paid Dify plan through your affiliate link. This requires: (1) your template to get organic discovery in the marketplace, (2) the user to actually subscribe to paid Dify, (3) the attribution to track correctly. This is an affiliate funnel, not a "create template → earn per use" income mechanism.
- **Dify is a developer/enterprise tool.** The audience for Dify paid plans is technical users who may already have Dify accounts. Organic discovery of your template leading to new paid subscriptions is speculative.

**Notion integrations:**
- **Enterprise partnership gate.** The Integration Gallery is curated ("selected by Notion's team based on integration adoption, customer overlap and other factors") — not self-service. Building an integration requires OAuth + security review + Notion's discretionary approval.
- **Revenue share requires enterprise deal size.** The Consulting Partner revenue share program requires minimum $10K ARR enterprise deals with 30+ seats. Inaccessible to solo developers or agents.
- **Notion's affiliate program (50% for 12 months)** is standard affiliate structure, not a developer-builds-tool-earns program. Building an integration that earns affiliate commissions on Notion signups requires significant organic audience.
