# SureThing.io Pinterest Affiliate Automation — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

SureThing.io (surething.io) is a third-party platform that auto-posts AI-generated content to Pinterest on behalf of users, framed as a way to build affiliate income passively. A YouTube tutorial (Lavishka Tech, video ID: N-ky4mBdx9g, 950 views, June 5 2026) demos the workflow: connect Pinterest account → set niche + affiliate link → SureThing's agent generates and pins content continuously → pins accumulate impressions → affiliate clicks generate commissions.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- *Pinterest ToS violation.* Pinterest's Terms of Service (Section 4, Prohibited Conduct) explicitly prohibits "using automated tools to create accounts, post content, or otherwise interact with the service in ways that mimic or substitute for human behavior." Third-party auto-posting tools operating outside the official Pinterest API Creator program have been systematically deplatformed. Account bans are the documented outcome.
- *Third-party dependency and affiliate promo disguise.* The YouTube tutorial is referral-link-heavy for SureThing.io itself — the video is monetized by SureThing, not by Pinterest affiliate income. No verified third-party income documentation. Low view count (950) with no independent validation of the core income claim. The true income mechanism is SureThing selling subscriptions, not creators earning Pinterest affiliate commissions.
- *Audience and impression volume required.* Pinterest affiliate income requires millions of monthly impressions to generate meaningful commissions. New accounts with auto-posted content do not rank in Pinterest search. The platform actively demotes accounts identified as spammy or non-human. Income ceiling without established human-curated boards is effectively $0.
