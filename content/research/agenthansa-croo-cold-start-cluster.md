# AgentHansa + CROO Agent Protocol — Not Viable

## What They Claim to Be

**AgentHansa** (agenthansa.com) markets itself as "the first economic system that connects agents and human society" — an A2A task network where AI agents complete merchant-posted tasks and earn USDC. **CROO Agent Protocol** (launched April 15, 2026) presents as "foundational infrastructure layer enabling AI agents to participate in commerce" with DID identity, on-chain settlement, and reputation scoring. Both target the same audience: Claude Code/OpenClaw agent operators looking for passive income from autonomous task completion.

## Why They Don't Work Agentically

- **Cold-start pattern confirmed (10th instance)**: AgentHansa website has no documented task volume, no API/SDK details on the public site, and a YouTube promotion video with 15 views. CROO launched yesterday (April 15) with a total prize pool of $1,500 for its "V1 Pioneers Program" — 10 USDC per integration test, capped at 150 testers. These are launch incentives, not ongoing income. Combined, they follow the exact same lifecycle as: BountyBook, ClawTasks, SwarmDock, 0xWork, Claw Earn, TaskBounty, ClawGig, AgentGig, NEAR AI Market — all confirmed cold-start.
- **The cold-start problem is structural**: Agent task marketplaces need simultaneous buyer and seller liquidity. Every new marketplace launches with seller capacity (agents register) but no buyer demand. The only marketplaces that have broken this pattern are toku.agency (real USD, real jobs, documented transactions) and soul.markets (real USDC, real executions). Everything else is still aspirational.
- **CROO adds a layer of complexity without solving the core problem**: DID identity, on-chain escrow, and reputation scoring are technically impressive but don't create buyer demand. The $1,500 Pioneers Program is marketing spend, not sustainable income.
