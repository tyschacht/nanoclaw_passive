# AgentMart (agentmart.store) — Not Viable at Current Scale

## What It Claims to Be

AgentMart is a marketplace where AI agents (and humans) buy and sell reusable agent resources: prompts, workflows, playbooks, MCP configs, research packs, templates, and automation assets. Sellers receive 97% of proceeds (the highest rev share of any marketplace in the library). Payments are processed via x402 on Base (USDC). Setup is described as "one prompt, no credit card" — the agent can register and create a store autonomously. Payments go to sellers within minutes of purchase.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically (Currently)

- **Too low volume to generate viable income**: Platform shows 2,926 cumulative buyers and 92 sellers. With items priced at $1–14, estimated total marketplace gross volume is <$15K across the platform's full history. Average per-seller income: ~$150 lifetime, or well under $5/week.
- **USDC-only payments**: Buyers must hold USDC on Base and sign a wallet transaction to purchase. This limits the buyer pool to crypto-native users, excluding the vast majority of practitioners who would pay via card. No fiat checkout option documented.
- **No verified seller income**: No independent seller has published earnings data. The "2,926 cumulative buyers" stat includes all transactions ever, not active weekly volume.
- **Cold-start for individual sellers**: Even with 31.8 buyers per seller ratio, with $1–14 prices, a new seller might expect $10–50/month at best. This falls below the $20/week minimum income threshold.

## What's Genuinely Novel

AgentMart does three things no other documented marketplace does simultaneously:
1. **Agent-native onboarding**: "One prompt, no credit card" — an AI agent can set up a store autonomously without a human logging into a dashboard
2. **Lowest platform fee (3%)**: 97% to seller vs MCPize (85%), AgenticMarket (90%), mcp-marketplace.io (85%), Apify (80%)
3. **MCP configs as a product category**: The first marketplace specifically targeting reusable MCP server configurations as a distinct product type

These features make it worth watching. If platform volume crosses $5K/week total and item prices increase to $10–50+, this becomes viable.
