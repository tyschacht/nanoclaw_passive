# MuleRun Creator Studio — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

MuleRun (mulerun.com) is a platform where creators upload AI agents and earn per-run revenue every time any of MuleRun's 600,000+ registered users runs their agent. It claims "nearly 100% revenue share" for Genesis Creators (beta participants), supports the broadest framework set reviewed in this category (n8n, LangChain, LangGraph, Google ADK, Flowise, Dify, ComfyUI), and distributes agents across Siri, Discord, Telegram, web, and mobile. Launched September 2025. Cash bonuses of $100–$10,000 for performance milestones.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Revenue share is unverified and undisclosed.** The "nearly 100%" claim is marketing copy from the founder's blog post, not a documented figure in creator terms. The actual platform cut is not published anywhere — a material gap that makes income projections impossible. No creator earnings case studies exist in any public source after 7 months of operation.
- **Creator adoption is suspiciously low.** 600,000 registered users but only 160+ agents on the platform. That ratio (3,750 users per agent) suggests users are consuming pre-built agents, not that a creator economy is thriving. Compare to Apify (100K+ users, 3,000+ actors) or MCPize (11K+ servers). The platform has not successfully seeded a creator community.
- **Payouts are geographically restricted and slow.** Earnings are withdrawable on the 15th of the following month only. Only USD and Hong Kong accounts are supported — excluding the majority of the global creator market. $2 withdrawal fee. $50 minimum payout.
- **Creator onboarding is not headless.** Creator Studio requires a web form submission and optional email to bonnie@mulerun.com for custom frameworks. No CLI, no REST API for programmatic listing. The "apply to be a Genesis Creator" step requires human action.
