# Base44 Super Agent SMB Automation Service — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Use Base44's "Super Agent" feature (launched March 2026) to build niche-specific AI automation workflows for small businesses — fitness coaches, real estate agents, course creators — and sell them as recurring $99-$299/month subscriptions. The agent is built once in Base44 (no code required; describe in plain language → AI builds the workflow) and runs 24/7 connecting to Google Sheets, Gmail, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Calendar, and CRMs. The pitch from Mikey No Code's April 2026 video (18K+ views): "five powerful AI agent use cases that can generate real income."

The five use cases demonstrated: (1) Fitness coach client accountability agent, (2) Payment/invoice reminder agent, (3) Course completion engagement agent, (4) Upsell/cross-sell agent, (5) Real estate lead closer agent.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Client acquisition is irreducibly human**: Building the Base44 Super Agent takes 15-30 minutes. Finding, convincing, and onboarding the fitness coach or real estate agent who pays you $199/month is 5-20 hours of human sales work per client. This is a freelance service business using AI to accelerate delivery — not agentic passive income. The same "client acquisition bottleneck" that kills AI research reports, ElevenAgents voice bots, and Google Reviews lead intel applies here identically.

- **No Base44 marketplace for organic discovery**: Base44 has a creator marketplace feature request (Under Review, 1 upvote) and an App Template Marketplace, but there is no published evidence that organic buyers discover and purchase pre-built Super Agents or templates at meaningful volumes. Without inbound discovery, every new client requires human outreach.

- **The Base44 "Agent Store" is conceptual**: The Anvita/Base44 documentation mentions an "Agent Store" but this is early infrastructure, not a live marketplace with buyer traffic.
