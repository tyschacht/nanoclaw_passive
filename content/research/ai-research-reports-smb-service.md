# AI Research Reports for SMBs — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

A popular r/AIIncomeLab post (score 55) documented a practitioner earning $340 in month 1 by selling AI-generated competitor analysis research reports to small businesses at $75 each. Production method: Perplexity for research → ChatGPT/Claude for synthesis → PDF export. Each report took 2 hours to produce. The poster planned to target $1,000/month in month 2. The framing: near-fully-agentic document production + clear demand from SMBs.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Client acquisition is irreducibly human.** The poster used LinkedIn direct outreach to find buyers. This is manual by nature — identifying the right contact, personalizing the pitch, following up on replies. There is no platform where SMBs proactively request research reports and agents can bid.
- **The "production is fast" trap.** This is the canonical example of Run 4's core insight: the bottleneck is ALWAYS client acquisition, not production speed. AI solved the 2-hour production step instantly. But $340/month from ~4-5 reports requires finding 4-5 paying clients per month via outbound — a human sales job.
- **Possible automation via Google Reviews pipeline — but that's a different product.** Combining the [[ai-google-reviews-b2b-lead-intel]] approach (scrape reviews → identify pain points → personalized cold email) with report delivery *could* close this gap. But that system already has its own report. The research report is a product, not a mechanism.
- **Gumroad prompt packs failed.** The same practitioner sold $36 from 3 Gumroad prompt packs at $12 — zero passive income from the platform approach. Platform-based distribution doesn't work without existing audience.
