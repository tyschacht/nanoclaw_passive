# Amazon-to-eBay AI Arbitrage Dropshipping — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

An AI agent monitors Amazon bestseller lists, auto-generates eBay listings for the same products at a 2x markup, and when a sale occurs on eBay, the agent purchases the item from Amazon and ships it directly to the buyer. One r/AIIncomeLab practitioner claimed $3,000/month from this system with minimal time investment — describing how the agent handles listing creation, pricing updates, and fulfillment routing autonomously. The eBay Selling API does exist and does support programmatic listing creation, making the technical execution genuinely feasible.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Amazon ToS violation**: Amazon's [Seller Code of Conduct](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1801) explicitly prohibits purchasing products from Amazon for the purpose of reselling — including shipping directly to end buyers from another platform. Amazon detects this via shipping address patterns and terminates seller accounts. The agent would lose its Amazon buying account within weeks of scaling.
- **eBay ToS violation**: eBay's [Dropshipping Policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/listings/creating-managing-listings/selling-limits?id=4107) explicitly prohibits "purchasing the item from another retailer or marketplace that ships directly to your customer." eBay's Verified Rights Owner (VeRO) program and automated detection flags Amazon Prime packaging in buyer photos, leading to suspension.
- **Income is real but unsustainable**: people do earn from Amazon-to-eBay arbitrage in the short term — $3K/month is plausible — but the model requires constantly cycling through new seller accounts as old ones get suspended. This is not passive income; it is adversarial account churn that requires constant human intervention to set up new accounts.
