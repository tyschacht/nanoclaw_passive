# Zoom App Marketplace — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

The Zoom App Marketplace has 1,000+ apps and integration with Zoom's 300M+ daily meeting participant base. Zoom introduced developer app monetization specifically to "create a new revenue stream for Zoom developers." Apps can be priced as one-time purchases or subscriptions with billing handled through Zoom's platform.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Developer keeps only 15%**: Zoom takes 85% of app revenue — the most punishing rev share of any marketplace in the library. Compare: Atlassian Forge (0%), Chrome Extension (95%), JetBrains (85% to developer), Figma plugins (85% to developer), Stripe App Marketplace (97% to developer). Zoom's model inverts the norm. At $20/month per user, the developer earns $3/month. You'd need 167 paid users just to hit $500/month.
- **Submission is manual**: App submission goes through Zoom's Marketplace Dashboard, not a programmatic CLI or API. Initial human setup is required.
- **Zoom meeting apps = narrow use case**: Apps must function inside a Zoom meeting or as a companion to Zoom workflows. This restricts viable AI utility categories compared to general-purpose marketplaces (Discord bots, Chrome extensions, Figma plugins).
