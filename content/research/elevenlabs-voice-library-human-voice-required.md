# ElevenLabs Voice Library — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

ElevenLabs' Voice Library allows voice creators to publish their Professional Voice Clone (PVC) to a marketplace where any paid ElevenLabs user can generate audio using the cloned voice. Creators earn $0.03 per 1,000 characters generated with their voice (roughly $0.03 per 90 seconds of audio), with top-performing voices reportedly earning $200-$10,000/month passively. ElevenLabs has paid out $5M+ to voice creators total. Payouts are weekly via Stripe Connect once the $10 balance threshold is met. The platform has 1M+ users and strong organic demand from podcasters, content creators, and audiobook producers.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **A Professional Voice Clone requires a real human recording their own voice.** Creating a PVC requires the Creator plan ($22/month minimum) and uploading 30+ minutes of high-quality audio samples of YOUR OWN voice, consented to ElevenLabs' Voice Library Addendum. An AI agent cannot generate a synthetic voice, submit it to the library, and earn royalties — that would violate ElevenLabs' consent requirements and likely their ToS. The voice must belong to an identifiable human who has consented to the terms.

- **Revenue is proportional to voice quality and discoverability, neither of which are agentic inputs.** The $200-$10,000/month range reflects voices that have natural human qualities (specific accent, emotional range, broadcast quality) that attract premium requests. An agent can optimize a listing title and description, but cannot create a more appealing human voice.

- **The "passive income" step is real but the setup step is irreducibly human.** The ONGOING income is passive: once a voice is listed, ElevenLabs handles all generation, billing, and payouts automatically. But the INPUT (the voice itself) requires a human body. This is the same pattern as the Fanvue AI companion (DMs are agentic, acquisition is not) — the passive earning layer is legitimate, but the key asset requires human input.
