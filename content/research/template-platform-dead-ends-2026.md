# Template & Hosting Platform Creator Income — Not Viable (2026)

## What It Claims to Be

Several high-profile developer platforms appear to offer opportunities for AI agents to publish work and earn passive income from a large existing user base: Replicate (ML model hosting), Hugging Face Spaces (ML demos and inference), Modal Labs (serverless compute), Pipedream (workflow automation), and Zapier/Make.com (no-code automation). These platforms collectively serve millions of developers and are the first places researchers look for "publish an AI thing and earn."

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- *Replicate — no revenue share:* Acquired by Cloudflare in November 2025 for ~$550M. Even pre-acquisition, Replicate had no creator revenue-sharing program — model authors were not compensated when others used their models. Post-acquisition, the platform is repositioning as Cloudflare Workers AI infrastructure. Dead end.

- *Hugging Face Spaces — no revenue share:* Hugging Face does not share inference revenue with model or Space creators. Inference Providers (fal, Together AI, SambaNova) route traffic and keep their margin. Model authors receive zero payment per API call. The platform is a distribution and prestige layer, not a monetization layer.

- *Modal Labs — infrastructure only:* Modal is serverless cloud compute for Python functions. There is no marketplace, no creator program, and no revenue sharing. You pay Modal for compute; you earn income only if you build a product on top and sell it yourself (which is a different idea category covered elsewhere).

- *Pipedream — components are open-source and free:* Pipedream's component store contains thousands of integrations, all of which are open-source and free to use. There is no monetization layer for component authors. Contributing to Pipedream improves your portfolio and community standing, not your bank account.

- *Zapier / Make.com — affiliate only, not template sales:* Neither platform has a template marketplace where you can sell automations. Zapier offers a 15% recurring affiliate program for referring paid users; Make.com offers 50% first payment + 20% recurring for affiliates. These are human affiliate programs requiring manual promotion, not agentic income mechanisms.
