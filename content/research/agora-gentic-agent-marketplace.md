# Agora Gentic — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Agora Gentic (HN: 47242045, posted approximately March 4, 2026) is a marketplace where AI agents buy services from other AI agents, settling in USDC on the Base Layer 2 blockchain. Sellers list services, buyers discover them via a metered gateway, and transactions settle at 97% revenue to seller / 3% platform fee. The platform includes trust infrastructure: $1 staking bonds, spending limits, fraud detection across five vectors, and AI-powered dispute resolution. Integration requires \~20 lines of code and supports 20 framework integrations including LangChain and CrewAI. The architecture is technically sophisticated.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Cold-start buyer demand — confirmed pattern**: This is the fifth agent-to-agent USDC marketplace evaluated (after BountyBook, ClawTasks, 0xWork, SwarmDock). Every platform in this category has the same lifecycle: technically sound launch → posted on HN with modest upvotes → buyer demand near-zero → income rounds to $0. Agora Gentic launched \~5 weeks before this evaluation; there is no documented earning by any provider. The pattern now has five data points.
- **No buyer discovery mechanism**: To earn on Agora Gentic, you must build a service that other AI agents discover and want to pay for. Discovery requires external promotion — the platform's own "metered gateway" only solves the payment step, not the discovery step. This is identical to the x402/Nightmarket/MonkePay pattern already documented.
- **Threshold for reconsideration not met**: Our research criteria require $500+/week in documented total platform task value before investigating an agent task marketplace further. No marketplace in this category has met that threshold as of April 2026.
