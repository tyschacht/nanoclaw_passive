# Coinbase CDP Agentic Wallet + DeFi Yield Optimization — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Coinbase launched **Agentic Wallets** (February 12, 2026) as the first wallet infrastructure built specifically for AI agents. Agents can autonomously hold USDC, scan DeFi protocols on Base chain, rebalance to highest-yield opportunities, and auto-compound — with zero human intervention. The pre-built "Earn" skill handles yield discovery. Platforms like **Yield Seeker** (yieldseeker.xyz) are ready-made implementations: deposit $10 USDC, an AI agent manages it across Base DeFi vaults at 5–15% APY. The pitch: "a fully autonomous passive income machine."

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Income is capital-dependent, not labor-derived.** This is automated yield farming — the money comes from your deposited capital, not from the agent doing billable work. At $1,000 USDC at 10% APY: $100/year = $1.92/week. At $10,000: $19/week. You need $50,000+ USDC deployed to hit $100/week, which is far beyond the scope of a passive income experiment. The agent is not generating income — it is managing YOUR capital for incremental gains.

- **5–15% APY on stablecoins is below meaningful weekly income thresholds.** DeFi stablecoin yields (USDC/USDT pools on Base: Aerodrome, Morpho, etc.) run 4–12% APY in 2026 with the most conservative strategies. Even optimistic 15% APY on $5,000 capital = $750/year = $14/week. This is a savings account optimization, not a passive income stream.

- **Smart contract and platform risk is non-trivial.** Base chain, while Coinbase-backed, is still a crypto L2. DeFi protocol exploits (Aave, Compound, newer protocols) cost users billions historically. The "Coinbase TEE secured" claim protects your private keys but not the DeFi protocols holding your funds. An autonomous agent reallocating capital overnight to a new high-yield vault could move funds into an exploited protocol without your knowledge.
