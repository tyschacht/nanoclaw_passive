# Hermes Agent "5 Ways I Make Money" — Not Viable

## What It Claims to Be

Hermes Agent (hermes-agent.org, GitHub: NousResearch/hermes-agent, 95.6K GitHub stars as of June 2026) is an open-source AI agent framework with persistent memory and tool-use capabilities. A cluster of high-view YouTube videos — most prominently Sharbel A.'s "5 Ways I Make Money With Hermes Agent" (2WZAcWtwoDI, 41,791 views, May 18 2026) and multiple follow-up videos — frame Hermes as a way to generate autonomous income. The framing attracted 40K+ views and generated numerous "how to make money with Hermes" secondary articles and blog posts.

The claimed methods include: cold email outreach automation, social media content scheduling, AI trading strategies, client communication workflows, and trend-monitoring alert subscriptions.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Creator explicitly disavows passive income framing.** In the same video, Sharbel says: *"Hermes does not remove the need for a real offer. It does not remove the need for you to figure out distribution. It does not remove the need for you to talk to customers. Not some magic money printer. Not some trading bot that secretly makes me rich while I sleep."* This is the author describing their own income methods. All 5 methods require human involvement at the value-generating step.
- **All 5 methods are AI-assisted freelancing, not autonomous income.** Method 1 (cold email outreach): human approves before sending. Method 2 (social content scheduling): human reviews content quality. Method 3 (trend alert subscription): the viable extraction from this video — see [[agentic-niche-alert-subscription]] which IS viable as a standalone product. Method 4 (AI trading): human approves trades (no live P&L shown). Method 5 (client communication automation): human approves outbound messages to clients. Every value-generating step requires human judgment.
- **"5 Ways to Make Money With AI"** videos are a saturated content category. Algorithms surface them heavily regardless of signal quality. The view count (41K+) is inflated by keyword demand, not by actionable passive income mechanisms. Any video with a high view count in this category should be evaluated by asking: "Does the creator need to work weekly for the income to continue?" In all 5 Hermes methods, yes — the income stops if the human stops working.
