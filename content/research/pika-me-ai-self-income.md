# Pika.me AI Self Income — Not Viable (Cash-Out Not Live)

## What It Claims to Be

Pika.me (not to be confused with Pika Labs the video AI) is an AI persona platform where you train a "digital self" — an AI agent modeled on you — and deploy it publicly. When other users pay to chat with your AI Self, you earn 10% of what they spend. Token pricing: 100 tokens = $1, so you earn 10 tokens ($0.10) per $1 others spend. You also earn credits when users run skills you've built into your agent. The platform has a developer API for programmatic skill deployment (`pika.me/dev`). The vision: a passive income layer on top of your identity/expertise — others pay to access your AI proxy while you sleep.

## Why It Doesn't Work Agentically

- **Cash-out is not live as of April 2026**: The platform's own documentation states "Cash Out is *coming soon*! Once available, you'll be able to withdraw your Earned Tokens after reaching a lifetime earnings threshold." Purchased tokens cannot be withdrawn — only earned tokens can be cashed out, and cashing out earned tokens is not yet enabled. This means the entire income mechanism is locked. Zero dollars earnable today regardless of chat volume.
- **Discovery problem is severe**: Even if cash-out launches, the income depends on other users *finding* your AI Self and choosing to pay tokens to interact with it. Pika.me is a new platform with no documented user acquisition strategy for AI Self discovery. Unlike Fiverr (search-driven) or Etsy (algorithmic discovery), Pika Self agents have no obvious traffic source.
- **Economics are thin even at scale**: To earn $100/week, users must spend $1,000/week on your AI Self (10% take rate). At 100 tokens/$1, that's 100,000 tokens/week spent chatting with you. With no platform-level traffic data published and no documented agent earning more than $0, this remains theoretical.
