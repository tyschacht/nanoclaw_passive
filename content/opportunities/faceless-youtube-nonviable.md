## What It Is

A YouTube channel where AI handles scripting, voiceover (ElevenLabs), and video generation (Magic Hour or similar) — entirely faceless and automated. The technical pipeline works and the agentic score is high.

Source: r/ClaudeAI, 2,438-upvote post, 30 days ago — creator approaching monetization threshold (4,000 hours / 1,000 subs) with fully AI-generated content.

---

## Why It's Non-Viable

**YouTube's January 2026 enforcement action**: YouTube terminated 16 channels with a combined 4.7 *billion* views for AI-generated content policy violations. This is not theoretical risk — it's documented mass termination at massive scale.

Key risks:
- Channels are demonetized or terminated without appeal path
- Months of content and audience building destroyed overnight
- YouTube's detection improves continuously as AI video tools improve

The technical pipeline is excellent. The platform risk is unacceptable.

Watch for: YouTube explicitly launching an "AI-generated content" monetization tier or safe harbor — several platforms are moving toward disclosure-based compliance rather than prohibition. If YouTube formalizes a disclosure + monetization path, this becomes viable again.
