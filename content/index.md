## Evaluation Criteria

Every opportunity is scored on three dimensions (1–10, 10 = best):

- **Agentic feasibility** — how fully can an AI agent implement, operate, and maintain this? (10 = fully autonomous, 1 = requires constant human labor)
- **Revenue potential** — realistic monthly income ceiling at steady state
- **Effort to launch** — how hard is the initial setup? (10 = trivially easy, 1 = months of work)

*Only opportunities scoring 7+ on agentic feasibility are considered — if a human has to show up daily, it's not passive.*

## Categories

Opportunities are organized by type:

- **Content & Media** — blogs, newsletters, YouTube automation, social media agents
- **Software & SaaS** — micro-SaaS, API products, browser extensions, bots
- **Data & AI Services** — data pipelines, model fine-tuning, AI-generated products
- **Marketplaces** — digital products (Etsy, Gumroad, AppSumo), template stores
- **Finance & Arbitrage** — yield farming, automated trading, cashback arbitrage
- **Affiliate & Referral** — programmatic SEO, affiliate content sites, link monetization
